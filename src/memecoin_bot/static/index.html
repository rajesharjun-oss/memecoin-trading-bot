    Server-Enforced Policy

    Risk limits

    These values are loaded from .env when the dashboard starts. The interface cannot silently weaken them.

    To change a limit: stop the dashboard, edit .env, then restart it. Keep live trading disabled until repeated dry-runs behave as expected.

    Irreversible Action

    Submit this trade to Solana?

    This sends a signed mainnet transaction from your burner wallet. Slippage, fees and a complete loss remain possible.

    Paper Portfolio

    Close this simulated position?

    The bot will request a current Jupiter exit route and close at its quoted value. Paper tokens do not exist in the wallet, so exits are priced rather than submitted or simulated on-chain. This changes only the local paper journal.
